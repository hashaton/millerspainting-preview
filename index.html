Services

Houses and condos, inside and out. Prep and cleanup are part of every job.

Exterior painting

Stucco and block walls, trim, doors, shutters, soffits, fascia and metal roofs. Florida sun and rain are hard on paint, so most of the work is in the prep.

Included
  • Pressure washing
  • Stucco cracks patched
  • Primer where needed
  • Finish coats and cleanup

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors and built-ins. One room or the whole house.

Included
  • Floors and furniture covered
  • Holes and dings repaired
  • Cut-in lines along trim and ceilings
  • Cleanup at the end of the job

Cabinet painting

Kitchen cabinets, islands and bathroom vanities. A lot less than replacing them.

Included
  • Cleaning and sanding
  • Primer for a lasting bond
  • Smooth finish coats
  • Kitchens, islands, vanities

Ceilings and drywall

Patching, water stains and full ceiling repaints.

Floors and decks

Garage floor and pool deck coatings, deck and porch staining.

Doors

Front doors, French doors and garage doors.

Repairs

Cracked stucco and damaged trim fixed before painting.

About us

Millers Painting is owned by Dennis and Robin Miller, a husband and wife in Port St. Lucie. We've been painting homes since 1992.

Dennis does the estimates himself and is on the job when the work is being done, usually with his son. When you call the number on this page, you reach us.

We use Benjamin Moore paint. We'll paint the colors you pick, and we keep you posted on the schedule, including when weather pushes a day back.

Common questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. Dennis comes to the house, looks at the job with you and gives you a price.

Who actually does the painting?

Dennis, usually with his son and crew. The person who gives you the estimate is the one on site.

What paint do you use?

Benjamin Moore.

Do you paint condos?

Yes. We paint houses and condos, inside and outside.

Can I use my own colors?

Yes. Pick as many as you want for walls, trim, doors and accents.
